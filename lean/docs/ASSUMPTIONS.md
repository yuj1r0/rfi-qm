- Jet increments are explicit tangent/jet data. A jet identity becomes a theorem about fields only after the needed chain-rule and realization results are proved.
- Division, derivatives, and integrals in Lean are totalized. Each use requiring nonzero denominators, differentiability, or integrability must carry the relevant hypotheses or an already proved lemma.
